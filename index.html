Birla Dharamshala Ayodhya – Affordable Stay Near Ram Mandir

Birla Dharamshala Ayodhya is one of the most preferred budget accommodations for pilgrims and tourists visiting the holy city of Ayodhya in Uttar Pradesh. Situated at Ayodhya Road, Sai Nagar, just a short distance from the world-famous Ram Janmabhoomi Temple (Ram Mandir), our dharamshala provides a clean, comfortable, and spiritually enriching stay at affordable prices.

Whether you are a solo devotee seeking a dormitory bed at ₹250, a family looking for an air-conditioned room at ₹1250, or a group needing a 4-bed room at ₹2100 per night, Birla Dharamshala Ayodhya has the perfect accommodation for every budget and requirement.

Why Birla Dharamshala Ayodhya is the Best Choice for Pilgrims

Ayodhya is not just a city — it is the birthplace of Lord Shri Ram and one of the seven holy cities (Sapta Puri) in Hinduism. With the grand inauguration of Ram Mandir, millions of devotees are visiting Ayodhya every month. Finding clean and reliable accommodation near Ram Mandir at an affordable price is a top priority for most pilgrims.

Our dharamshala is known for its cleanliness, security, and peaceful environment. We provide free WiFi, free parking, hot water, and 24-hour security — all at no extra cost.
